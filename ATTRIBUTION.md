## Meshes
No downloaded 3D assets are used. Every mesh is generated by the scripts in `tools/`:
- `bake-character.mjs`: the driver (grandpa), sculpted from signed-distance primitives,
  polygonised with surface nets, decimated and skinned to a 60-bone skeleton with a face rig.
  Skin and plaid shading are procedural.
- `bake-fisherman.mjs`: the standing fisherman of Driver POV, built the same way.
- `bake-truck.mjs`: the white extended-cab pickup, a generic early-2000s style full-size
  pickup with no manufacturer badges or logos.
- `bake-props.mjs`: the cap on the dashboard and the first-person hand.
- Trees, grass, reeds, pond, road, guardrail, rod, reel, wheels, glass, bucket and fish are built at runtime
  in `src/`.

## Audio
- **Dialogue:** short excerpts of the original meme's audio, cut, faded and loudness-normalised.
  Nothing was generated or voice-cloned. Driver POV and Fish POV use three longer continuous excerpts with
  both voices, keeping the original pauses and interjections.
- **Invented lines** (answers off the original script) use a "blah-blah" mumble synthesised in
  the browser; it imitates nobody.
- **Sound effects and ambience** (birds, wind, water, engine, cast, reel, line strain, creaks,
  tyre scrape, thuds) are synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API in `src/audio/audio.js`.

## Likeness
The characters are stylised recreations of the people in the original meme video, made for these
parody games. The video itself is not part of the games or this repository.
